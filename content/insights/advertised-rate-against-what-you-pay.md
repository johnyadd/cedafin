Bank of Ghana requires every bank to report the annual percentage rate on its
lending — the rate a borrower actually pays once fees, commissions and charges
are included. It publishes the results.

Most Ghanaian borrowers never see them. What they see is the rate on the
poster, in the branch, on the website.

Those are not the same number.

## The gap

Bank of Africa's one-year SME loan is reported to the regulator at a minimum of
13.70% and a maximum of 23.42%.

| | Rate |
|---|---|
| Advertised, minimum | 13.70% |
| **What it can cost** | **23.42%** |
| **The gap** | **9.72 points** |

The lower figure is real — some borrowers get it. But a business owner reading
13.70% and budgeting on it may find the loan costs almost twice that, and
nothing on the way in tells them which end of the range they are at.

That gap is fees. The interest rate is one component; arrangement fees,
commitment fees, processing charges and insurance make up the rest, and the APR
is the only figure that captures all of it.

## What SME credit costs across the market

Twenty-two banks report. For a one-year SME loan in May 2026:

| Bank | APR |
|---|---|
| **Standard Chartered** | **11.03%** |
| Ecobank Ghana | 13.01% |
| First Atlantic Bank | 13.51% |
| Bank of Africa | 13.70% |
| ... | |
| GCB Bank | 27.54% |
| **Guaranty Trust Bank** | **33.58%** |

The cheapest and the dearest are 22.55 percentage points apart, for the same
product, in the same market, in the same month.

On a GH₵100,000 loan over one year that is roughly GH₵22,550 — the difference
between a bank that lends and one that is barely worth borrowing from.

## Only three of twenty-four mention a rate anywhere

We checked the websites of every bank we track. Three mention a rate. The rest
publish product descriptions, eligibility criteria and application forms, with
no figure at all.

That is not a regulatory failure — Bank of Ghana collects and publishes the
data. It is a disclosure gap between the regulator's publication, which few
borrowers read, and the bank's own material, which they all do.

## What a borrower can do about it

Ask for the APR, not the interest rate. It is the figure the bank already
calculates and reports to the regulator every month, so it exists and can be
quoted.

Ask which end of the range you fall in, and why. A bank reporting 13.70% to
23.42% knows where a given applicant sits before it lends.

And compare before applying. The difference between the cheapest and dearest
one-year SME loan in Ghana is larger than the entire cost of borrowing from the
cheapest.
